## Creative moves
1. **Background = story thermometer**: yellow (happy sale) → coral (wrong number) → crimson (no way to track) → violet flood exactly on «اما» with the music drop.
2. **The padlock is the gate**: it slams shut on «قفل» and only opens when the green «تأیید» coin flies into it.
3. **Dead end → open road**: the tracking path that hits a wall at 9.76 s comes back at 30 s with the wall gone and the box reaching the house.
4. **Sound story**: original music bed is thin & minor through the problem, a riser, then the full major groove drops on «اما راه حلش».
5. **Kinetic word**: «اشتباه» glitches & shakes in red; «دردسر» balls literally bounce off the shield.
